About

I'm Md. Foysal Bin Mamun, an aeronautical and aviation science engineering student based in amp; Dhaka, Bangladesh. My work sits at the intersection of aircraft systems and computation — from assisting on the restoration of a MiG-21 to co-authoring a machine learning study on magnetohydrodynamic nanofluid convection.

Outside coursework, I completed an international research internship in Electrical & Electronics Engineering at the Manipal Institute of Technology under IAESTE India, and I stay active as a long-distance runner, cyclist, and karateka — currently holding medals from over 30 running events across Bangladesh.
